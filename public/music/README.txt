AURASOUND — LOCAL MUSIC FILES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Drop your MP3 files into this folder (public/music/).
Vite serves them as /music/<filename>.mp3 automatically.

REQUIRED FILENAMES (must match exactly):
──────────────────────────────────────────
  happy.mp3       ← Happy mood  (Track 1) — e.g. Pritam upbeat song
  happy2.mp3      ← Happy mood  (Track 2)
  happy3.mp3      ← Happy mood  (Track 3)

  sad.mp3         ← Sad mood    (Track 1) — e.g. Arijit Singh
  sad2.mp3        ← Sad mood    (Track 2) — e.g. Anuv Jain
  sad3.mp3        ← Sad mood    (Track 3)

  neutral.mp3     ← Neutral mood (Track 1) — e.g. Anuv Jain lo-fi
  neutral2.mp3    ← Neutral mood (Track 2)

  angry.mp3       ← Angry mood  (Track 1) — e.g. Dark Phonk
  angry2.mp3      ← Angry mood  (Track 2)

  low.mp3         ← Low mood    (Track 1) — Devotional / God songs
  low2.mp3        ← Low mood    (Track 2)

  surprised.mp3   ← Surprised mood (Track 1)

HOW THE LOW MOOD WORKS:
──────────────────────────────────────────
  'Low' is a custom AI mood — NOT a standard face-api expression.
  It triggers automatically when the AI detects:
    sad score ≥ 0.30  AND  neutral score ≥ 0.30 simultaneously.
  This plays your devotional/God songs playlist.

TIPS:
──────────────────────────────────────────
  • Use MP3 format for best browser compatibility.
  • 128kbps or higher recommended for good audio quality.
  • File size doesn't matter — they're served from localhost.
  • To update songs: replace the .mp3 file, no code changes needed.
